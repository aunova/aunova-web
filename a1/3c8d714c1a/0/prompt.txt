## What needs to change, in one paragraph

The current page positions Greenblocks as "community impact infrastructure" with the audience implicitly being municipalities, residents, and any developer who cares about sustainability. That was the right framing six months ago. Post-REES, the positioning has consolidated around something sharper: Greenblocks is a Digital Building Passport for premium real estate, deployed at asset level, that turns developer claims into verifiable data. The audience is Dubai premium residential developers as the direct commercial client, with buyers, investors, and regulators as the surrounding stakeholders the passport serves. The page needs to reflect that consolidation while keeping enough of the systems-thinking warmth to distinguish Aunova from generic PropTech.

Eight sections below, each with the current copy and the proposed replacement.

## 3. Problem section

### Current
- H2: *Modern communities are structurally blind.*
- Body: *People live in buildings, but not in communities. Daily life produces environmental, health, and social consequences, yet these remain largely invisible. Cities and developments today operate without real infrastructure to: understand their environmental impact, support healthy living by default, connect daily behavior to long-term outcomes, cultivate real community life. Sustainability becomes reporting. Health becomes an individual struggle. Community becomes accidental. These are not separate problems. They are symptoms of the same missing system. What is missing is community impact infrastructure.*
- Three tiles: *Isolated Sustainability / Disconnected Health / Fragmented Community*

### Proposed
- H2: *Premium real estate has a credibility gap.*
- Body: *Developers invest heavily in sustainability, wellness, design quality, construction integrity, and community life. Buyers and investors hear the claims. But during early-stage sales and beyond, almost none of it is visible, verifiable, or comparable across assets. The market is no longer asking for better claims. It is asking for proof.*
- Three tiles:
  - **Marketing without verification** — Claims buyers cannot independently confirm.
  - **Reports without infrastructure** — Performance data reduced to brochures and PDFs.
  - **Reputation without measurement** — Brand built on narrative, not provable substance.

Four tiles, reframed around what the passport delivers commercially:

- **Premium positioning** — Trust beyond marketing. Reputation as a measurable asset attribute, not a brand exercise.
- **Buyer comparability** — Projects easier to compare on substance, not branding.

## 7. Partnership model

### Current
- H2: *Partnership model*
- Body: *We collaborate with forward-thinking developers, municipalities, and organizations to create sustainable, connected communities that thrive.*
- Three principles: *Selective / Long-Term / Mission-Aligned*

### Proposed
- H2: *How we deploy*
- Body: *Greenblocks is deployed at asset level, with the developer as the direct client. The first deployment becomes the reference case. From there, the passport extends across additional assets and the wider portfolio. We are currently deploying in Dubai with premium residential developers.*
- Three principles:
  - **Asset-level, not portfolio-wide:** One specific building. One reference case. Scale follows proof.
  - **Reference-case logic:** The first deployment sets the standard for what verified premium real estate looks like.
  - **Partners, not vendors:** Long-term collaboration, co-built outcomes, no feature checklists.
